# 🌍 International Freight & Multimodal Transportation Analysis

**Strategic Trade-Off Modeling: Transit Time vs. Total Cost of Transportation across Global Lanes**

An executive transportation and logistics decision framework evaluating ocean, intermodal rail & over-the-road (OTR) models for international freight routes servicing Europe & North Africa.

---

## 🎯 Executive Summary & Problem Statement
Global supply chain networks require continuous optimization to balance inventory carrying costs against rising freight tariffs. This analysis evaluates trade-offs across key international trade lanes servicing manufacturing and distribution nodes (e.g., Tijuana, DFW, Chicago, Long Beach, Houston) to European hubs (France, Germany, UK) & North Africa (Tunisia).

---

## 📊 Key Evaluation Lanes & Scenarios

### 1. Transatlantic Ocean vs. Intermodal Rail/Ocean Trade-Offs (Europe & North Africa)
* **France (Mouguerre):** Evaluated **All-Boat** (Tijuana $\rightarrow$ Long Beach $\rightarrow$ Le Havre $\rightarrow$ Mouguerre | 50 Days, $7.14K) vs. **Intermodal Rail** (Tijuana $\rightarrow$ Houston Rail $\rightarrow$ Le Havre | 38 Days, $11.09K). Identified a **12-day transit time reduction** at a 55% freight rate premium.
* **Germany (Freiburg):** Modeled **All-Boat** (via Long Beach & Hamburg | 59 Days, $7.10K) vs. **Intermodal** (via Houston & Antwerp | 38 Days, $9.90K)[cite: 4]. Strategic trade-off analysis established a business case for rail due to a **21-day reduction in supply chain lead time**.
* **United Kingdom & Tunisia:** Assessed route viability through Southampton and Genoa-Rades, ruling out intermodal shifts where lead time gains were negligible compared to rate spikes.

---

## 🔑 Key Strategic Impact
* **Inventory Lead Time Reduction:** Pinpointed specific lanes where a 21-day reduction in transit time economically justifies intermodal rail freight premiums.
